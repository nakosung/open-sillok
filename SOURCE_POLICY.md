# Original sources and rights

The source institution is the National Institute of Korean History (NIKH). Each prepared source links to `https://sillok.history.go.kr/id/ARTICLE_ID` and records its retrieval date, exact original-text SHA-256 fingerprint, raw-page fingerprint, and observed reuse mark. Source texts are the Classical Chinese original sections, not the modern Korean translation sections. Korean article titles and archival date labels are retained as identifying metadata.

The original-text pages display `공공누리 공공저작물 자유이용허락`. The archive's [2025 service and rights update](https://sillok.history.go.kr/intro/people.do) distinguishes original/scan reuse from modern translation rights. Preserve source attribution, reuse evidence, and direct links. Open Sillok is independent and is not endorsed by NIKH. Its translations are newly produced and are not the archive's official English edition.

Do not infer a blanket license covering every component of a retrieved page. Do not commit page HTML, modern Korean translation bodies, official English translations, or scans into public contributions. Raw HTML is kept outside the repository only for bounded collection and independent source matching. The source collector must remain rate-limited and restricted to explicit article IDs. Further bulk ingestion requires a separately reviewed collection plan and a check of then-current access and reuse conditions.

Source changes require a separate maintainer PR with the official URL, evidence of the changed passage, renewed fingerprint/retrieval metadata, and an explanation. Translation PRs cannot change sources. A changed source makes previous translation provenance fail until it is checked and updated, and old independent review hashes no longer apply.

The current candidate manifest contains 600 discovered IDs. It is not a complete catalog of the annals. Published translations, prepared untranslated sources, candidate IDs, and the eventual total corpus are separate counts. The pilot target is 500 translations; no claim of full coverage is made.
