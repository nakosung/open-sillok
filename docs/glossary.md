# Working terminology

This is a starter glossary, not a historical dictionary. Propose contextual changes with source evidence. Preserve an original term when its precise institutional sense is unresolved.

| Source term | Starting convention | Context to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 實錄 | Veritable Records; annals in running prose | The compiled record and its individual statements are distinct. |
| 上 | the king | A contextual reference, not part of a personal name. |
| 史官 | historiographer | An official recording role. |
| 經筵 | royal lecture; royal lecture session | Consult the surrounding verb and institutional context. |
| 是月 | this month | Monthly scope, even if filed under the month's last day. |
| 閏 | intercalary | Lunisolar month, not a Gregorian leap day. |
| 奴婢 | enslaved people; male and female slaves where explicitly distinguished | Preserve household, state/private, and sex distinctions where given. |
| 啓 | reported; submitted a report to the king | Preserve the reported speaker and claim. |
| 傳旨 | royal instruction | Do not assume a modern statute. |

Units, ambiguous office names, and unverified personal-name readings should remain explicit rather than receive unsupported modern equivalents. Expand this glossary through separate, reviewable PRs; do not change the source text.
